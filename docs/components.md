# Component inventory

What to reuse before building anything new. This is step 1 of **Reuse first** in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), and it applies to specs and plans as much as to code.

**How to check**

1. Find the job below and use the pick.
2. Not listed? Search `src/components/` and the feature's folder under `src/app/` for it: the name you'd give it, its ARIA role (`role="dialog"`, `role="tab"`), or the words on screen ("No open tasks").
3. Close but not quite? Extend it with a prop or variant. Don't fork a copy.
4. Nothing fits? Research it on Mobbin, build it in `src/components/` if it's general-purpose, and add a row here in the same commit.

Paths are under `src/`. Last full sweep: 2026-10-23.

## Forms

| Job | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Text field | `components/ui.tsx` `Input` | `--surface` on a `--line-control` edge; focus turns the edge `--act` with a 1px inset ring, `aria-invalid` turns it `--danger`. `text-body`, so 14px with a mouse and 16px on touch (iOS doesn't zoom on focus). `size` `md` 36px (the default) or `sm` 32px (filter bars, table rows, rails), at least 44px on touch. `prefix` and `suffix` put a unit inside the edge in `--fg-3` ("$", "%", "sq ft"); the text keeps clear of it and the unit is read through `aria-describedby`; with a slot, `className` goes on the wrapper. `fieldClass` is the look for a field that can't be `Input`; `fieldSizeClass` its heights |

- **Tap targets.** The primitives are 44px on touch now. Raw `<button>`s and fields outside `ui.tsx` (filter bars at `h-8`, text links in tables) still warn; move them onto the primitives.
- **Sticky headers on wide tables.** A table wider than its column scrolls sideways in its own box, and a header can't stick to the page from inside a scroll container, so there `Th` scrolls away with the page. Sticking it needs a header synced to the box's scroll.
- **Loading state.** `Spinner` and `Button` `pending` exist, and the command palette draws static `--fill-1` rows shaped like its finished ones while a first load runs (`SkeletonRows` in `command-palette.tsx`). The page-level one is the Financials Overview's `(overview)/loading.tsx` (labels drawn at once, static `--fill-1` blocks at figure height, `MonthBarsSkeleton`): copy its pattern, in the route's own group so sibling tabs don't borrow its shape. Older pending actions still relabel.
